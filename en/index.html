FAQ

FAQ

Which areas can I inquire about?

Inquiries currently focus on Yunlin and Chiayi. Contact us directly to confirm the location and arrangements. Private addresses are not published on this site.

How do I book and confirm the fee?

Call 0912-202-025 or prepare an inquiry on the appointment page. Details, time, location and fees are confirmed by both parties in advance.

How do the four techniques differ?

Pressing emphasizes stationary contact; kneading uses circular rhythms; gliding follows a direction; delicate localized movements involve finer contact. Ask about the actual service arrangements in advance.

Can I discuss pressure beforehand?

Discuss the contact area, comfort and preferred pressure in advance. If you feel uncomfortable during the session, tell the provider immediately and stop the relevant movement.
