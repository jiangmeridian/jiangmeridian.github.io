BEFORE YOUR VISIT

Preparing for your first relaxation session

Begin with booking, privacy, contact areas and communication about pressure to establish clear expectations.

Jiang Meridian Wellness | Website editorial draft · November 26, 2026

Ask for details before decidingRespect your own contact boundariesComfort does not mean stronger is betterWhen uncomfortable, understand the service boundaries first

Ask for details before deciding

Before booking, ask about the available services, time, location and fees. Without explicit confirmation, completing a website form does not constitute a confirmed appointment.

Respect your own contact boundaries

Contact areas, clothing and covering arrangements should be discussed and agreed in advance. If you have concerns about any arrangement, you may suggest another choice or decline it.

Comfort does not mean stronger is better

Everyone has different tolerances for touch. If you feel uncomfortable during a session, speak up and stop the relevant movement; there is no need to endure it.

When uncomfortable, understand the service boundaries first

Non-medical traditional bodywork does not provide diagnosis, treatment or disease-related consultation. For acute injury or persistent or severe unusual sensations, first seek help from a qualified healthcare professional.
