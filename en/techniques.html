Pressing and kneading: differences in contact rhythm

Pressing illustrates steadier contact, whereas kneading shows circular variations. Observe the rhythm and contact area without seeking a particular pressure.

Gliding and fine touch: differences in movement

Gliding illustrates a more continuous direction of movement; fine touch shows smaller changes in contact. Different names do not imply fixed effects; discuss comfort first.
