Fragrance-free spaces can also be comfortable

Create an easy place to rest through lighting, cleanliness, materials and personal preferences, without relying on scented products.

Fragrance is not essential to a wellness atmosphere

Eastern aesthetics may evoke incense, tea settings and plants, but fragrance is only one sensory element. Wood grain, window light, fabric textures and a tidy space can also convey a quiet lifestyle. A space without added fragrance may better suit those who prefer subtle scents. This is an environmental choice, not a promise of health benefits.

Start with the cleanliness of objects

Regularly dust surfaces and objects, wash used textiles and keep items dry. If an obvious odor is present, identify its source instead of masking it with strong fragrance. Consider scent and intended use when choosing cleaning products; a fragrance-free label does not mean a product contains no ingredients or has no restrictions.

Create visual depth

Warm wooden furniture can be paired with off-white fabrics, muted greens, simple ceramics or ink paintings. Rather than filling the room with decorations, leave space to move and place objects. If you prefer not to add fragrance, choose decor without an obvious scent and consider its materials and cleaning needs.

Agree on fragrance-free shared spaces

Family and visitors may experience scents differently. In shared rest areas, agree not to introduce strongly scented products unless discussed together. If someone wears perfume or uses other personal products, calmly explain your preference and find an arrangement acceptable to both sides without asking about their medical background.

Fragrance-free does not mean pollution-free

Taiwan's Ministry of Environment notes that indoor air management also requires attention to pollution sources and ventilation. Even without added fragrance, outdoor air, cleaning products and furniture materials can affect air quality. Fragrance-free is not an air quality test result and cannot replace necessary cleaning, ventilation or other safety measures.

Start with a rest corner

Choose a location that does not obstruct family members, with a stable chair, lighting suitable for reading and room for a glass of water. Clear unnecessary items, keep the area safe and see whether you enjoy it. Begin with existing furniture; no extra purchases or uniform style are required.

Simplify gradually instead of renovating at once

Remove one unnecessary source of fragrance first and observe after a few days. If furniture or fabrics have a strong smell, handle them according to their material and instructions. There is no need to pursue a completely odorless standard if the arrangement meets your needs.

FAQ: Can I request a fragrance-free setting when booking?

Yes. State your preference and ask whether the actual setting and products can accommodate it. The website does not publish a fixed service address or confirm every facility's equipment. Clarify special environmental requirements before arranging a visit and book only after mutual confirmation.
