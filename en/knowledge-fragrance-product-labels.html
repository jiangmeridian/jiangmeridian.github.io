Fragrance product labels and everyday purchasing

Check purpose, source, expiry and precautions before purchasing; distinguish room fragrance products from cosmetics.

Start with intended use, not bottle appearance

Fragrance products may be intended for rooms or cosmetics for external use on the body. Attractive bottles do not make them the same category. Before buying, check intended use, target users and precautions. Do not use poorly documented products based on internet hearsay.

Visible sources make further checks possible

Check product name, responsible supplier contact, ingredients, use and storage. Labeling rules differ by category; requirements for one cosmetic cannot be extrapolated to all fragrance products. If online information is insufficient, request the complete package label from the seller before deciding.

Check opening and storage conditions

Taiwan FDA advises checking purpose and expiry of fragrance cosmetics and watching for deterioration. Keep original packaging or record opening time; avoid decanting into unlabeled containers. If contents are unidentifiable, past expiry or noticeably changed in appearance or smell, do not continue using them.

Do not treat promotional adjectives as safety guarantees

Natural, botanical, pure or premium does not prove suitability for everyone. Higher prices do not establish medical or other special effects. Distinguish sensory preference, product information and safety. Unverified effect claims should not justify purchases or replacing medical care.

Avoid mixing products across uses yourself

Do not apply room products to skin because they smell good or add fragrance products to food or drinks. This article offers no essential-oil ratios, blends or application instructions. If labeling is unclear, check with the responsible supplier rather than applying another product's directions. Those concerned about ingredients may choose not to use it.

A practical purchasing sequence

First decide whether a purchase is needed, then compare purposes, packaging information and restrictions before scent and price. For a comfortable room, light, ventilation, storage and cleaning may eliminate the need for fragrance. If unused products already exist at home, do not buy another set merely for a theme.

Read complete information when shopping online

If a product page shows attractive bottles and scent descriptions but lacks purpose and precautions, request package information rather than guessing from other buyers. On receipt, check that it matches the description at purchase and retain necessary transaction records.

FAQ: Does Jiang recommend these products?

No. This is general purchasing and information literacy guidance, with no specified brand, product, price or recommended formula, and no claim that Jiang sells or supplies fragrances. Any future product information should be separately verified according to category and applicable requirements.
