Explain the service direction you are interested in

Briefly explain the body area for everyday relaxation you wish to discuss, available times and contact preferences. Technique and body-area articles are general knowledge, not a fixed service menu. Ask about fees and time in advance

Before confirming, ask about expected duration, total fee, other necessary costs and cancellation, rescheduling or lateness policies. No fixed public price list is currently provided, so do not infer Jiang's prices from other businesses or online articles. Keep mutually confirmed information in your own contact records for later reference.

Location depends on mutual agreement

The website does not publish a private address or promise a fixed studio or home visits in advance. First confirm whether the area can be served, then agree on the detailed location and transportation. Do not travel to an unverified address. Ask about the venue, companions or privacy before deciding whether to book.

Provide only necessary contact information

For an initial inquiry, do not enter sensitive information such as identity numbers, medical records or detailed addresses on public pages or general forms. Start with your preferred name, region, availability and what you wish to discuss. If other information is required, confirm its purpose and transmission method first. Seek medical assessment for health concerns.

Final confirmation before booking

Before departing or making other arrangements, reconfirm date, time, place, service details, fees and the contact method for rescheduling. Express preferences for contact areas, pressure or draping beforehand. Do not assume unconfirmed conditions; postponing a booking is reasonable if both sides cannot agree.

Summarize key conditions in one confirmation message

After discussion, restate the agreed date, time slot, region, service and cost in your own words and ask the other party to confirm any differences in understanding. Obtain mutual agreement again for changes. Do not apply old message times to new arrangements or infer the venue from website imagery.
