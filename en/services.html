YOUR COMFORT FIRST

From body area to sensation: find the right inquiry topic

Even after an equally busy day, people with different lifestyles may have different expectations for rest.

Rest for the upper body

After prolonged computer use, reading with the head lowered or desk work, describe the neck, shoulders and upper back you wish to relax, as well as places you do not want touched.

Rest for the lower body

After standing, walking or commuting, discuss rest needs around the lower back, hips, legs and feet. Confirm the actual contact areas and methods together.

Privacy and covering

Ask about clothing, covering methods and venue arrangements before booking. Any touch requires the person's consent and may be stopped at any time.

Communicate before deciding

Time, fees, actual services and location must be confirmed. For unexplained or persistent discomfort, seek medical evaluation first.
