Gliding: direction and continuous contact

Understand direction, contact area and continuity, and the difference between viewing a film and actual experience.

Observe how contact extends from one place

In the four brand illustrations, gliding mainly shows continuous directional contact. Unlike pressing pauses or localized kneading changes, viewers can notice how contact begins and ends. Direction is only an external movement feature, not the path of a particular meridian, organ function or bodily effect. No uniquely correct path can be determined from a video.

Continuous does not mean fast or intense

Some prefer long, steady contact, others shorter contact with more pauses. Movement from start to finish may appear smooth, but the camera cannot measure pressure. Recipients need not accept an unwanted rhythm to match an illustration. Request slower motion, altered areas or a stop at any point during everyday relaxation rather than waiting until the end.

Confirm the area before discussing technique

For gliding inquiries, first ask which areas will actually be contacted, whether a towel is used and what must be confirmed in advance. Boundaries for broad contact differ between individuals. Prior agreement does not prevent changing one's mind later. State any areas you do not wish touched; the provider should respect that choice.

Do not mistake illustration art for anatomy

The site's East Asian ink and flowing-line decorations express brand aesthetics, not internal anatomy. Videos are AI illustrations, not instruction records based on actual observation. Compare the pressing, kneading and plucking pages to learn differences; do not infer bodily functions from arrows, colors or effects. Direct questions about specific physical conditions to qualified healthcare professionals.

A session need not follow a complete routine

Before booking, describe desired relaxation areas and time and agree on available arrangements. If alertness, time or feelings differ that day, discuss them again. Watching four videos does not require all four techniques or a particular sequence as a necessary body-care procedure. Safety, respect and clear arrangements are shared prerequisites.

Ask separately about time and contact area

For example, with only a short break, confirm available time rather than asking for more content at greater speed. To inquire about broader contact, ask whether clothing or covering must change. These are advance arrangements unrelated to whether direction produces a specific effect.

FAQ: Must a technique follow a direction to be effective?

The site claims no effects based on direction and provides no personalized path instructions. Gliding direction is only a clue to its style. Appropriate providers should arrange actual technique within legal service boundaries and on-site communication; recipients may refuse unsuitable touch. Stop if uncomfortable and seek medical assistance as needed.
