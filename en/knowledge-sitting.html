EVERYDAY MOVEMENT

Desk work and everyday movement

Make brief periods of standing, rest and activity part of your day instead of waiting until work is finished.

Jiang Meridian Wellness | Website editorial draft · November 26, 2026

Begin by observing everyday situationsFit movement into existing habitsOrganize your workspace tooMake room for rest

Begin by observing everyday situations

When using a computer, driving or watching a series, we often maintain the same posture without noticing. Instead of demanding a perfect sitting posture at all times, notice how long you have stayed in one position and whether your desk and chair make it easy to stand up.

Fit movement into existing habits

Taiwan's Health Promotion Administration recommends increasing opportunities to interrupt prolonged sitting. Drinking water, getting documents, answering a phone call or waiting for the next work stage can be reminders to stand up. Choose safe, comfortable movement and build the habit gradually.

Organize your workspace too

Place frequently used items within easy reach and adjust the chair and screen to a comfortable height so rest and movement are easier. If you work for long periods, you can agree on short breaks with colleagues.

Make room for rest

After a busy period, make time for a walk, quiet rest or an activity you enjoy. Manual relaxation is an optional leisure activity and cannot replace everyday movement or medical care.
